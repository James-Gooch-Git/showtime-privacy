Showtime Support

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Showtime is an event and riding-order management app for equestrian competitions. If you need help using the app, have a question, or want to report a problem, we're here to help.

Contact Us

The fastest way to reach us is by email:
info@showtimehorses.co.za

Please include the following so we can help you faster:

  • A description of the issue or question
  • The device and operating system you are using (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 17)
  • The app version, if known
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, if reporting a bug

Response Time

We aim to respond to all support enquiries within 2 business days.

Common Topics

  • Account help — trouble signing in, resetting access, or updating your details
  • Events & entries — entering events, viewing riding orders, and schedules
  • Bug reports — anything not working as expected
  • Data requests — to access or delete your data, see our Privacy Policy

Privacy

For details on how we handle your information, please read our Privacy Policy.
